## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a serene, contemporary boutique hospitality sanctuary rooted in Central Java's heritage. The brand personality is calm, understated, sophisticated, and deeply hospitable. It avoids ostentatious luxury in favor of mindful architectural pauses, generous whitespace, natural textures, and artisanal warmth. 

The aesthetic marries **Tactile Minimalism** with **Warm Editorial Modernism**. Large evocative photography capturing natural daylight, teak textures, and linen sets the stage. Geometric simplicity is counterbalanced by soft, pill-shaped tactile controls, airy typographic hierarchy, and barely-there Javanese batik motifs applied with ultra-low opacity (4–8%) as watermarks and sectional dividers. The interface creates an unhurried, restorative cadence that invites guests to pause and immerse themselves in the spirit of Java.

## Colors

The palette is derived from natural Indonesian architecture, earth pigments, and tropical flora:

- **Deep Teal (`#123C3A`)**: The primary brand anchor. Used for primary interactive triggers, immersive dark background blocks, deep headers, and grounding footers. Accompanied by **Teal Dark (`#0C2B29`)** for pressed states and hero overlays.
- **Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`)**: The secondary tone. Introduces architectural warmth, supporting alternating content strips, interactive hover washes, and secondary button backgrounds.
- **Brass Gold (`#B88A44`)**: The tertiary accent. Reserved exclusively for precise visual cadence: active navigation pips, subtle category eyebrows, micro-borders, and focus rings. Never used as large solid fills.
- **Ivory (`#FAF7F2`)**: The foundational canvas neutral. Replaces harsh digital white with an organic, linen-like surface.
- **Surface White (`#FFFFFF`)**: Pure elevation card backgrounds and crisp interactive controls.
- **Text & Structure**: **Charcoal (`#1F2523`)** delivers high-contrast readability (meeting WCAG AAA standards against Ivory), while **Stone (`#5E6663`)** softens secondary metadata. **Mist (`#E2DCD0`)** provides low-contrast, hairline separation.
- **Functional Semantics**: **Botanical Green (`#2F7D5B`)** for booking successes, **Terracotta (`#B5472F`)** for validation alerts, and **Soft Amber (`#FFF4DC`)** strictly for demo mode indicators.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Playfair Display** (editorial, classical, graceful) with **Inter** (functional, highly legible, modern).

- **Headlines & Section Titles**: Set in Playfair Display with weights 500 to 600. Tight line heights (1.1–1.3) maintain architectural proportion in large sizes, while negative tracking gives hero headlines an editorial posture.
- **Body & Paragraphs**: Inter set with an open line-height (1.7) to reinforce the relaxed, airy temperament of the sanctuary.
- **Eyebrows & Architectural Labels**: Set in Inter semi-bold, 12px, fully capitalized with wide letter spacing (`0.12em`), frequently tinted in Brass Gold to delineate thematic transitions.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon a balanced fluid grid system:
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column layout with 20px (`1.25rem`) lateral padding and 16px (`1rem`) gutters.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px)**: 8-column layout with 32px (`2rem`) padding and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters.
- **Desktop (≥ 1024px)**: 12-column layout capped at a max-width container of `1200px`, centered with 40px (`2.5rem`) outer margin and 24px (`1.5rem`) gutters.

Rhythm is deliberate and unhurried:
- Inter-section vertical rhythm uses `72px` (`space-3xl`) on mobile and expands up to `120px` (`space-4xl`) on desktop to maintain breathing room.
- Each section adheres strictly to the single-focus principle: a unified message per screenful, preventing visual clutter.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is conveyed through **Organic Ambient Shadows** and **Tonal Tiering** rather than synthetic floating layers:

- **Shadow Character**: Low-opacity, diffused, and organically tinted with Deep Teal:
  - *Standard Card Elevation*: `0px 12px 32px rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.06)`
  - *Floating Interactive (e.g., Availability Bar, WhatsApp FAB)*: `0px 16px 40px rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.12)`
  - *Hover State*: Cards subtly elevate with `0px 20px 48px rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.10)` combined with a 2px upward shift.
- **Surface Layering**: 
  - Level 0: Ivory canvas (`#FAF7F2`)
  - Level 1: Elevated cards and booking widgets on pure White (`#FFFFFF`) with Mist (`#E2DCD0`) hairline borders (`1px`).
  - Level 2: Dialog overlays and mobile navigation drawers layered over a 40% Deep Teal tinted backdrop blur (`backdrop-blur-md`).
- **Texture Accents**: Background regions utilize SVG Javanese batik filigrees rendered between 4% and 8% opacity, creating subtle tactile dimensionality without interfering with content contrast.

## Shapes

The design system employs a refined curvature hierarchy that balances architectural structure with tactile softness:

- **Inputs and Form Controls**: `12px` border-radius for an approachable, natural feel.
- **Cards & Visual Containers**: `16px` border-radius (`rounded-lg`) on card surfaces to maintain neat containment.
- **Photography & Featured Modules**: `20px` border-radius (`rounded-xl`) on room and gallery imagery, softening expansive photos.
- **Interactive Action Triggers**: Fully rounded pills (`9999px`) on buttons and chips, offering high affordance and tactile contrast against the rectangular structural grid.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill**: Background Deep Teal (`#123C3A`), text Ivory/White (`#FFFFFF`), pill radius (`9999px`), padding `14px 28px`. Hover lifts `-2px` with Teal-tinted shadow. Active scales down `0.98`.
- **Secondary Pill**: Transparent background, `1.5px` border in Deep Teal, text Deep Teal. Hover fills with Warm Sand (`#EFE6D8`) at 40% opacity.
- **Ghost Action**: Unbordered, text Deep Teal, animated `1.5px` Brass Gold underline on hover.
- **Accessibility & Focus**: Keyboard focus triggers a distinct `2px` Brass Gold outline offset by `2px`.

### Availability Widget (Hero Dock)
- Elevated White container floating over the hero boundary, rounded `16px` to `20px`, ambient shadow `0px 16px 40px rgba(18, 60, 58, 0.10)`.
- Internal dividers styled with Mist (`#E2DCD0`).
- Seamless inputs with subtle icons in Brass Gold (`#B88A44`) and primary pill CTA ("Cek Ketersediaan").

### Cards (Rooms & Amenities)
- Base surface White with Mist `1px` border and `16px` radius.
- Imagery framed at `4:3` aspect ratio with `20px` radius, zooming `1.05x` gently over `400ms` on hover.
- Metadata row uses Stone (`#5E6663`) text with inline stroke icons (1.5px stroke weight).
- Brass Gold price emphasis ("mulai Rp... / malam").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- Height fixed at `52px` with `12px` radius.
- Borders default to Mist (`#E2DCD0`) against White background. Focus state transitions to Deep Teal border with an outer Brass Gold halo (`0 0 0 1px #B88A44`).
- Error state highlights with Terracotta (`#B5472F`) and inline icon alert.

### Chips & Badges
- Filter pills (e.g., in Gallery) use `36px` height, pill radius, Warm Sand background, and Charcoal text. Selected state switches to Deep Teal fill with White text.
- Demo Banner Chip: Soft Amber (`#FFF4DC`) pill with Charcoal text and Brass Gold hairline boundary.

### Selection Controls (Checkboxes & Radios)
- Checkboxes: `20px` square with `6px` radius. Border Mist default, transitioning to Deep Teal fill with white checkmark when checked.
- Radios: Circular `20px` with Deep Teal concentric dot indicator.

### Floating Action Button (WhatsApp)
- `56px × 56px` circular pill in Deep Teal, anchored at `bottom: 24px`, `right: 24px`.
- High elevation shadow (`0 12px 28px rgba(18,60,58,0.2)`), featuring a crisp white WhatsApp glyph.
